AUTONOMOUS DRONES WITH AI · GROUP PROJECT

Modelling and controlling a nonlinear quadrotor

Connecting vehicle dynamics, rotor behaviour and feedback control in MATLAB and Simulink.

From model to trajectory

Our group developed nonlinear quadrotor simulations, cascaded control, rotor models and trajectory-tracking tests. The model connected the vehicle’s physical response with feedback loops that guide it along a desired path.

This work strengthened my understanding of feedback and actuator dynamics, providing a practical foundation for my later dissertation on UAV modelling and learned residual corrections.
