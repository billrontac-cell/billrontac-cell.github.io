ADVANCED DIGITAL TWIN AND APPLICATIONS · GROUP PROJECT

Digital twin prototyping

A Unity-based fire-protection simulation prototype developed through group coursework.

My contribution

I contributed Unity development, 3D scanning, testing, demonstration videos and presentation materials. The project brought together a virtual environment and an application scenario to explore how a digital representation can support system understanding.

The result was a simulation prototype, rather than a live sensor-connected digital twin. My work focused on developing and testing the virtual experience and communicating its behaviour.
