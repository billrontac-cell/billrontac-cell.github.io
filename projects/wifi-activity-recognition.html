Exploring Wi-Fi activity recognition

Preliminary work on pose data and tools for a Wi-Fi-based human activity-recognition project.

An early research exploration

My preparation included reviewing a pose dataset and setting up DeepLabCut. This introduced me to the data and tooling needed to investigate human activity and pose estimation.

The project remained unfinished. It did not produce a validated end-to-end Wi-Fi activity-recognition system; the experience represented an initial exploration of datasets and experimental setup.
