BENG FINAL-YEAR PROJECT

PricePal: product search and price comparison

An Android prototype connecting product search, price comparison and a supporting data workflow.

Building a complete prototype

Supervisor: Professor Tee Sim Hui

I developed a Kotlin Android application with customer and administrator interfaces. A Python and BeautifulSoup workflow collected product listings, while Flask supported search and Firebase and SQLite handled application data, product administration and wishlists.

The prototype also integrated a ChatGPT API interaction for product queries. My work covered requirements, design, implementation and documentation, including the limits of scraping reliability and external APIs.
