MSC DISSERTATION · ONGOING

Hybrid neural models
for UAV dynamics

View project →

Combining physics-based quadrotor dynamics with learned residual corrections to predict how a drone responds to control commands.

Supervisor: Professor James Lam · HKU

PyTorchRotorPyPython
2.65%lower mean one-second position RMSE
across 32 held-out simulation scenarios and 3 training seeds
Research question, contribution & findings +

My work

I developed simulation and evaluation pipelines, explored six neural-model variants, and studied prediction across vehicle configurations. The refined study separates training, development and held-out scenarios, with frozen checkpoints and reproducible evaluation.

What I learned

Mean position RMSE decreased from 0.1659 m to 0.1615 m against the original physics baseline. Closed-loop tracking outcomes were mixed: improved prediction alone did not ensure improved control. I also investigated mass, inertia and wind variations, tracking error and command variation.
